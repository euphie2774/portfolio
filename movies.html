My Best Films and Shows

My personal movie list.

  1. Peaky Blinders (2013-2022) — Power, ambition, and revenge may bring success, but unchecked greed, betrayal, and violence ultimately destroy the very people who seek control.
  2. God Father of Halem (2019) — The series shows that true leadership is not simply about controlling people; it is about protecting your community and considering the consequences of your decisions.
  3. The Matrix (1999) — Never accept everything you are told as truth; question reality, seek knowledge, think independently, and have the courage to challenge systems that control your freedom.
  4. All the Queens Men (2020) — Ambition and power can build an empire, but greed, manipulation and betrayal can destroy trust, relationships and everything one has worked to achieve.
  5. Better Call Saul (2015-2022) — Dishonesty, shortcuts and the desire for success can gradually destroy your integrity, relationships and identity, while taking responsibility for your actions is the first step towards redemption.
